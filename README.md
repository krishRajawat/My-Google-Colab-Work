🛠️ The Projects

1. 🛡️ HealthGuard: The AI Caregiver

"Because every life is a majority class."

  - The Problem: In a hospital, 90% of patients are stable, but the 10% in
    critical condition need the most attention.
  - The Solution: A predictive system that uses Gradient Boosting to prioritize
    "Recall" over "Accuracy," ensuring that high-risk patients are detected
    early.
  - Tech: Python, Imbalance-Aware Boosting, Scikit-learn.

2. ☀️ EcoPulse: Solar Energy Forecasting

"Bridging the gap between the Grid and the Cloud."

  - The Problem: Weather is unpredictable, and the power grid needs to know
    exactly how much solar energy is coming.
  - The Solution: A specialized Random Forest Regressor that fuses atmospheric
    data with electrical power logic to provide real-time energy output
    forecasts.
  - Tech: Ensemble Learning, Sustainability Data, Matplotlib.

3. ⚡ High-Speed Anomaly Detection (CS-ELM)

"Math is faster than Gradient Descent."

  - The Problem: Traditional Neural Networks are slow to train on imbalanced
    data.
  - The Solution: A manual NumPy implementation of a Class-Specific Extreme
    Learning Machine (CS-ELM). It’s a non-iterative model that solves for
    weights analytically.
  - The Result: 10x faster training and high sensitivity to rare anomalies.
  - Tech: Pure NumPy, Linear Algebra, Pseudoinverse Theory.

4. 💰 The Financial Guardian: Cost-Sensitive Spam Filter

"Not all mistakes cost the same."

  - The Problem: Missing a spam email is annoying; marking a client's email as
    spam is a business disaster.
  - The Solution: An NLP-based Cost-Sensitive System that assigns higher
    penalties to "False Negatives," forcing the AI to think like a cautious
    business manager.
  - Tech: NLTK, TF-IDF, Cost-Sensitive Logistic Regression.

5. 🐚 EcoShell: Ecological Maturity Monitor

"Predicting the future of nature, one shell at a time."

  - The Problem: Identifying mature species in a dataset where "Juveniles" are
    the majority.
  - The Solution: A comparative study between Bagging (Balanced RF) and Boosting
    (XGBoost) to identify the most robust architecture for rare biological
    maturity cycles.
  - Tech: XGBoost, BalancedRandomForest, SMOTE-logic.
